Turn 2 · From the anti-feed spec

Two briefings a day, then closed

Retainer collects at 6:00 am and 6:00 pm, with no live refresh. Posts come one at a time with a fixed end, every post can say why it is there, and filters are corrected from the post itself. Counts and replies are removed before a post reaches you.
